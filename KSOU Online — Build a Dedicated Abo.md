KSOU Online — Build a Dedicated About Us Page

Create a completely dedicated About Us page for KSOU Online.

This is not another homepage section.

The page should tell the story of Karnataka State Open University, its history, scale, academic mission, accessibility, recognition and transition into online education.

The existing KSOU Online homepage and individual programme pages already establish the visual design system.

Continue that exact design language, but give this page a more editorial, institutional and storytelling-driven feel.

Before implementation, use the installed skills throughout:

ui-ux-pro-max
react-expert
frontend-design

Do not create a generic university “About Us” template.

The page should feel like a premium institutional profile.

From Open Learning to a Digital Future.

Content

Use the following structure:

1969

The Roots

1992

1996

KSOU

Today

Online Education
