Yes. I would **skip the video section for now** and move directly into **How Online Learning Works**.

I think this section should be very visual because the visitor should understand the entire process in **5–10 seconds**, without reading a wall of text.

## Recommended concept: “From Admission to Degree”

### Section title

**How Online Learning Works**

### Subtitle

**Everything you need to learn, complete your programme, and earn your degree — online.**

Then create a **horizontal journey/timeline** rather than normal cards.

## The structure

I would use **5 stages**:

### 01 — Apply & Get Admitted

**Start your journey online**

Application → document submission → admission confirmation.

**Icon:** FileCheck / ClipboardCheck

↓

### 02 — Access Your LMS

**Your classroom, anywhere**

Log in to the KSOU Online LMS and access your learning resources.

**Icon:** MonitorPlay

↓

### 03 — Learn & Engage

**Learn at your pace**

Access lectures, study materials, assignments and other academic resources.

**Icon:** BookOpen / GraduationCap

↓

### 04 — Complete Examinations

**Demonstrate what you've learned**

Complete your examinations through the prescribed KSOU academic process.

**Icon:** PenLine / FileCheck

↓

### 05 — Earn Your Degree

**Your achievement, officially earned**

Complete your programme requirements and receive your degree/certificate as applicable.

**Icon:** Award / GraduationCap

# But here's how I'd actually design it

Don't make five boring rectangular cards sitting next to each other.

Instead:

```text
             HOW ONLINE LEARNING WORKS
      Everything you need, from admission to degree.

        01              02              03
      APPLY  ───────→   LMS  ───────→  LEARN
        │                                │
        │                                │
        ↓                                ↓
        04 ───────────→ 05
     EXAMINATIONS          DEGREE
```

But visually, I'd make it much more sophisticated.

### Desktop

A **large horizontal pathway** across the section.

Each stage has:

**Number**
→ **Icon**
→ **Title**
→ **1–2 line explanation**

And a thin blue connecting line running through all five stages.

The connecting line itself can have a **small animated blue/gold travelling dot** moving from:

**01 → 02 → 03 → 04 → 05**

That gives the section a feeling of progression.

---

# Mobile

Don't force five tiny cards onto the screen.

Turn it into a **vertical journey**:

**01**
Apply & Get Admitted
↓
**02**
Access Your LMS
↓
**03**
Learn & Engage
↓
**04**
Complete Examinations
↓
**05**
Earn Your Degree

The connecting line stays on the left.

This would look much cleaner than horizontally squeezing the process.

# Add one interesting interaction

I would make each step **expandable**.

For example:

### 03

**Learn & Engage**

When clicked:

> Access video lectures, study materials, assignments and other resources through the online learning environment.

You don't need to show all the information immediately.

This keeps the initial section clean while still allowing visitors to explore.

---

# Visual style

Keep it consistent with your existing KSOU design:

**Background:** White
**Text:** `#111111`
**Primary blue:** `#4169E1`
**Secondary accent:** Metallic gold

Use the gold **only for the progression indicator / tiny accents**.

For example:

**01** → Blue
connecting line → Light grey
active/progress dot → Gold

That would look much more premium than making everything blue.
