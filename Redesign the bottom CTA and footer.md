Redesign the bottom CTA and footer section of the KSOU Online website.

The goal is to create a premium, cohesive, institutional and visually intentional ending to the page.

The current design feels too vertically separated between the counsellor CTA and footer, and the CTA typography does not have a strong enough editorial composition. Redesign both sections as one connected visual experience, while still keeping the CTA and footer clearly distinguishable.

Use the existing KSOU Online visual identity:

Deep navy blue
Royal/medium blue
Very light blue/off-white
White
Extremely subtle muted gold accents where appropriate
Dark navy typography

The overall feeling should be:

Premium + Trustworthy + Modern University + Editorial + Human + Sophisticated

Do not make it look like a generic SaaS footer.

SECTION 1 — COUNSELLOR CTA
Reduce the unnecessary vertical gap

The biggest issue in the current design is the excessive empty space between the counsellor CTA and the footer.

The CTA should transition naturally into the footer.

Do not leave a large empty background area underneath the CTA.

The counsellor image should visually help bridge these two sections.

Desired composition

Use a two-column CTA:

LEFT: Text and CTA
RIGHT: Counsellor image

The counsellor image will be supplied separately.

Use the supplied transparent counsellor image.

The woman is wearing a navy blue formal shirt and headset.

Important image positioning

The counsellor image should be positioned toward the right edge of the CTA.

She should not simply sit inside a small image box.

Instead:

Allow the image to extend vertically downward toward the footer boundary.

The top of the counsellor should begin naturally within the CTA, while the lower part of the image should visually approach or overlap the upper boundary of the footer.

The image should feel like it is anchored into the transition between CTA and footer, similar to the first reference image.

Do not crop her face.

Do not make her appear unnaturally oversized.

Maintain a natural visual proportion.

CTA TYPOGRAPHY

The current heading:

Have Questions?
Connect With Our
Counsellor

feels too fragmented and unnecessarily tall.

Create a more sophisticated editorial hierarchy.

The heading should preferably be two lines, not three.

Use a composition such as:

Line 1:

Have Questions?

Line 2:

We're Here to Help

This is much cleaner visually.

Alternative if you want the university/counselling message to be more explicit:

Line 1:

Need Guidance?

Line 2:

Talk to Our Counsellor

Another option:

Line 1:

Your Questions,

Line 2:

Our Guidance.

I prefer "Have Questions? / We're Here to Help" for this particular CTA because it feels warm without sounding overly promotional.

Heading styling

Use a large editorial serif or sophisticated display typeface consistent with the existing KSOU Online website.

The heading should have:

Strong visual weight
Tight but elegant line height
Large size
Excellent contrast
Two-line composition
Controlled width

Do not allow the heading to span too wide.

The second line can be slightly visually stronger if appropriate.

CTA SUPPORTING TEXT

Under the heading, use a short, restrained sentence:

Have questions about programmes, admissions or online learning? Our counsellors are here to help.

Keep this to approximately 1–2 lines on desktop.

Do not create a large paragraph.

Use a smaller neutral navy/grey typeface.

CTA BUTTON

Use:

Talk to a Counsellor →

instead of the generic "Apply Now" inside this particular section.

The CTA here is about conversation and guidance, not directly forcing admission.

The button should use KSOU's primary blue.

Design:

Medium rounded corners
Strong contrast
Subtle hover animation
Small arrow
No excessive shadow

The button should feel premium rather than like an advertisement.

CTA BACKGROUND

Use a very subtle light blue/off-white background.

Do not use a strong gradient.

You may use extremely subtle abstract KSOU-inspired shapes in the background, but they should barely be visible.

For example:

Oversized curved line
Very subtle circular form
Soft architectural curve

Keep opacity extremely low.

The background decoration should never compete with the counsellor or heading.

CTA HEIGHT

Keep the CTA relatively compact.

Do not create a giant hero-like section at the bottom of the page.

The section should have enough breathing room but should transition naturally into the footer.

Suggested visual hierarchy:

CTA

↓ small controlled spacing

Footer

Not:

CTA

↓

huge empty area

↓

Footer

Clear hierarchy

IMPORTANT DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The bottom of the KSOU Online website should leave the visitor with three feelings:

1. "I can trust this."

Through the institutional KSOU connection.

2. "I can get help."

Through the counsellor CTA.

3. "I know where to go next."

Through the programme and resource navigation.

The design should communicate these things visually, without adding unnecessary text.

DO NOT DO

Do not:

Leave a huge gap between CTA and footer
Use a three-line oversized heading
Use generic "Have Questions? Connect With Our Counsellor" typography
Make the CTA unnecessarily tall
Put the counsellor inside a small rectangular card
Crop the counsellor's face
Make the footer overly tall
Add unnecessary footer categories
Make the main KSOU logo compete with KSOU Online
Use heavy gradients
Use excessive glassmorphism
Use excessive shadows
Use random decorative blobs
Use orange CTA buttons
Make every footer element rounded
Turn the footer into a SaaS-style mega-footer
Add unsupported institutional claims
FINAL DESIGN DIRECTION

The final result should feel like a premium government-university digital platform, not a commercial ed-tech startup.

The visual relationship should be:

Human counselling → Institutional trust → Clear navigation

The counsellor CTA provides the human connection.

The KSOU Online footer provides the digital platform identity.

The linked Karnataka State Open University identity provides the institutional connection and credibility.

Together, the CTA and footer should form a polished, compact and memorable end-of-page signature for KSOU Online.
