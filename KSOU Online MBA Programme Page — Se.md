NAAC A+

KSOU's official university material states that the university received NAAC A+ accreditation with a GPA of 3.31 on a seven-point scale, valid for five years from May 19, 2023.

Display this as a major trust badge rather than burying it among small logos.

Government University
State Government University

Karnataka State Open University

and clearly communicate that KSOU is a state university established under the Karnataka State Open University Act framework.

KSOU's institutional material identifies it as a public university established in 1996, with its roots in the earlier Institute of Correspondence Courses and Continuing Education.

Ranking / Positioning

The requested positioning is:

8th among Karnataka Government Universities

However, do not publish this as an unsupported ranking claim.

Before implementation, verify the exact ranking source and ranking methodology.

If the supplied official ranking source confirms the claim, display:

#8 among Karnataka Government Universities

with a small supporting line identifying the ranking source and year.

If the claim cannot be substantiated by an authoritative source, do not fabricate a ranking badge. Keep the space available for the verified ranking asset/content.

This is particularly important because the available KSOU prospectus and official materials I reviewed do not themselves establish that exact ranking. The search results I found also do not provide a reliable current ranking source for that specific claim.
